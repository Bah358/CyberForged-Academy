How Placement Works

Five Steps from Partnership to Hire

The simplest hiring process you will experience for cybersecurity talent.

1

Share Your Requirements

Tell us what roles you need filled, what certifications you require, and what technical skills matter most. A 20-minute conversation is enough.

2

We Align Our Curriculum

We adjust our training content to specifically match what you told us your roles require. Your graduates are trained for your environment, not a generic one.

3

Students Train and Build Their Portfolio

Students complete 6-16 weeks of practical training, building real deliverables — IR reports, risk registers, SIEM investigations — that demonstrate what they can do on day one.

4

We Pre-Screen and Present

We assess every graduate against your specific job requirements and present only the qualified candidates to you — with their portfolio attached. No sifting through unqualified CVs.

5

You Interview, You Hire, We Collect

You interview pre-screened, trained candidates. When you make an offer and they accept, you pay the placement fee. Not before.

Partnership Tiers

Three Partnership Levels

Start with Bronze — no commitment required. Move up as your hiring needs grow.

🥉

Bronze Partner

15% of year 1 salary

Access graduate profiles on request. No upfront commitment. No minimum number of hires. Pay only when you make an offer and it is accepted. The easiest way to start the partnership.

🥈

Silver Partner

12% of year 1 salary

Commit to 2 or more hires per year and receive priority access to graduates before they are presented to other companies. Your company logo appears on the CyberForged Academy website.

🥇

Gold Partner

10% of year 1 salary

5 or more hires per year — or sign an annual employee training contract. Receive input into our curriculum, dedicated recruitment sessions for your open roles, company-branded graduation certificates, and priority scheduling for corporate training.

All tiers include a 30-day replacement guarantee — if a graduate is not the right fit within 30 days, we replace them at no additional cost.
Graduate Competencies

What Our Graduates Can Do on Day One

Every graduate arrives with practical skills demonstrated through a real portfolio of deliverables — not just a certificate.

SOC Analyst Graduates

  • Investigate SIEM alerts end-to-end
  • Analyse Windows and Linux logs
  • Use EDR platforms for investigation
  • Write structured incident reports
  • Apply MITRE ATT&CK to real alerts
  • Conduct basic threat hunting

IR Graduates

  • Lead an incident response lifecycle
  • Preserve and document evidence
  • Conduct disk and memory forensics
  • Triage and analyse malware samples
  • Produce formal IR reports
  • Manage stakeholder communication

GRC Analyst Graduates

  • Conduct ISO 27001 gap assessments
  • Build and maintain risk registers
  • Write information security policies
  • Produce Statements of Applicability
  • Assess NIST CSF maturity
  • Prepare audit evidence packages

ISO Graduates

  • Design a security programme roadmap
  • Develop and track security KPIs
  • Present risk to board and executives
  • Lead incident response strategically
  • Manage security budgets
  • Respond to ISO interview scenarios

Tech Startups and SaaS

Healthcare and NGOs
