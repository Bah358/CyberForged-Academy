Security Awareness Training

Your most important training investment. Teaches all employees — regardless of technical background — to recognise and respond to the threats they face daily: phishing emails, social engineering, unsafe passwords, and data handling mistakes.

Delivered as a half-day or full-day workshop. Includes live phishing simulation demonstrations, interactive exercises, and completion certificates.

All employees Half or full day Arabic & English At your premises or Zoom Annual contract available

ISO 27001 & GRC Fundamentals

Designed for IT managers, compliance officers, and department heads who need to understand what ISO 27001 compliance actually requires — not just theoretically but in practice. Covers risk registers, control assessment, and what an audit looks like.

Prepares your team to work alongside GRC consultants, participate in audits, and understand compliance obligations under GDPR, PCI-DSS, and local regulations.

IT managers Compliance officers Full day (8 hours) ISO 27001 aligned

SOC & Incident Response Fundamentals

For your IT and security team members who need to detect and respond to threats. Covers SIEM basics, alert triage, the incident response lifecycle, evidence handling, and how to escalate correctly when something goes wrong.

Includes hands-on lab exercises using LetsDefend and CyberDefenders platforms. Students investigate real simulated alerts during the session.

IT security teams 2 days Hands-on labs included SIEM & EDR focus
