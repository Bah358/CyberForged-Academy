I founded CyberForged Academy because I saw the same problem every time I looked at the Lebanese cybersecurity market: companies cannot find trained candidates, and trained candidates cannot find a structured path into employment.

CyberForged Academy exists to close that gap — with practical hands-on training that builds real skills, a portfolio that proves those skills to employers, and a direct placement connection that removes the guesswork from job searching.

I teach from active practice, not from memory. Every course I deliver is informed by what I do daily as a GRC Analyst and what I see clients and employers actually need — not what a textbook written five years ago says they need.

Projects and Tools

What I Have Built

These are not theoretical exercises. Every project is a working product built from real GRC and security knowledge.

CyberGRC Assess

An AI-powered GRC assessment platform replicating KPMG's Cyber Maturity Assessment methodology. Evaluates organisations across 9 security domains mapped to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A and NIST CSF 2.0 — with AI-generated executive summaries and remediation roadmaps via the Claude API. Used in the GRC Analyst training track as a practical assessment tool.

github.com/Bah358/cybergrc-assess

SecureBank GRC Portfolio

A complete ISMS implementation for a fictional financial services organisation — demonstrating end-to-end GRC Analyst competency. Includes: Information Security Policy, 15-risk Risk Register (ISO 27005 methodology), Annex A Control Mapping (35 controls), NIST CSF 2.0 Gap Assessment (2.3/5.0 maturity score), and Vendor Risk Assessment. Used as the template for GRC track student portfolios.

github.com/Bah358

Early Warning SIEM

A Python SIEM prototype with real-time log ingestion, MITRE ATT&CK-aligned detection rules, and risk scoring. Includes a Streamlit SOC dashboard for alert visualisation. Used in the SOC Analyst track to demonstrate how detection rules are built and how alerts are generated from raw log data.

github.com/Bah358/SIEM-Prototype

USB Sentinel

A Python endpoint security tool that detects BadUSB and HID injection attacks using Windows Management Instrumentation (WMI) with automated PowerShell blocking of unauthorised USB devices. Demonstrates practical endpoint protection concepts used in the SOC and IR tracks.

github.com/Bah358/usb-sentinel
Why CyberForged Exists

Practical Skills. The Right Roles. A Clear Path to Employment.

CyberForged Academy was built around three things that no other training in Lebanon delivers together: practical training through real labs and real projects, a deliberate focus on the four cybersecurity roles with the highest demand and best salaries, and a direct connection to the companies hiring for those roles.

Most training stops at the certificate. We do not. We build you the skills, publish your work publicly so employers can see it, connect you directly to our partner companies, and give you a structured progression path from Level 1 through to senior — in the roles that actually hire at entry level and pay well from day one.

For companies, we do the same thing in reverse — we train your employees in exactly the areas your organisation needs, tailored to your industry and compliance requirements, delivered by a practitioner who is doing this work right now.
